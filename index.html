      Classes

      • Warrior: 10 HP, advantage vs Humanoids, 1 special use to gain a level, and takes 1 less damage from non-trap monsters.
      • Hunter: 9 HP, advantage vs Beasts, 2 special uses to reroll the current room, and gains Beast damage in levels when defeating Beasts.
      • Thief: 8 HP, advantage vs Giants and Traps, 1 special use to sneak past the current room without damage or level gain, and takes no damage from traps.
      • Cleric: 7 HP, advantage vs Undead, 1 special use that heals to full and defeats an Undead encounter, plus passive healing after every room up to HP + level.
      • Mage: 6 HP, advantage vs Fey and Fiends, 1 special use that casts a random spell with varied effects and recovers after each room.
